Design direction comparison for Lumina AI workspace

Recommended direction
Soft Brutalism — Neo-brutalist structure, muted tonal palette
Keep the bold borders and grid personality of neo-brutalism — that's your identity and what makes Lumina feel distinct from every other glassmorphism tool. Strip out the full-saturation colors and harsh whites. Darken the backgrounds to a warm near-black, replace neon with dusty amber and sage, and let the brutalist geometry breathe through subtle contrast rather than screaming contrast. The result: recognisably your app, but liveable for hours.
